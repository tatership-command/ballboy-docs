---
title: "How do I link my gamertag or stream links after I already own a team?"
summary: "Run /team connect <league> — it reopens the Activity's finish-linking view for a team you already own."
weight: 55
---

Run `/team connect <league>` — just the league, there's no `team` or `season`
option to fill in. It requires that you already own a team, and it launches the
same Activity used for claiming, pre-filled to the "finish linking" view
instead of the team picker, so you can update your identity source or stream
links without re-claiming.

This is a manual-mode-only command — in an EA-mode league, team ownership
follows the EA companion export instead. See
{{< relref "/docs/flows/claim-and-connect" >}}.
