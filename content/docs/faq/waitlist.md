---
title: "How does the waitlist work?"
summary: "/waitlist add/remove/promote/list — a commissioner-managed queue for members without a team."
weight: 52
---

If every team is claimed, a commissioner adds you to the league's waitlist with
`/waitlist add`. `/waitlist list` shows the current order — anyone with access
to the league can check it. Commissioners can also `/waitlist remove` someone
or `/waitlist promote` them to the front.

Being on the waitlist doesn't claim anything for you automatically. Once a team
opens up (someone leaves it, or a commissioner releases one), claim it yourself
with `/team claim`, or ask a commissioner to `/team assign` it to you directly.
See {{< relref "/docs/commands/waitlist" >}}.
