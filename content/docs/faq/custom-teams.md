---
title: "A team I need isn't in Ball Boy's 143-team list — can I add it?"
summary: "Yes — commissioners add a custom or missing team from the Custom Teams tile in the Activity."
weight: 133
---

Yes. Commissioners can add a school Ball Boy doesn't ship with (or a team
that's missing for some other reason) from the **Custom Teams** tile on the
Activity hub. Search by name — Ball Boy combines College Football Data and
ESPN to prefill a match with logo, colors, and conference, and every field is
still editable — or switch to the Manual tab and enter everything by hand. The
team is added as an unowned (CPU) team right away, so it's immediately
claimable like any other.

The same screen's Manage tab lets you edit or remove a custom team later. A
team already in a scheduled game can't be deleted until it's cleared from the
schedule, and deleting a claimed team releases its owner back to CPU control
first. A custom team can never shadow the name or abbreviation of one of Ball
Boy's built-in teams.
