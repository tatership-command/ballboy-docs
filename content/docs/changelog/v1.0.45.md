# v1.0.45 — Twitch Stream Auto-Detection

_Released 2026-07-26_

## What's New

**🔴 Twitch stream auto-detection.** Go live on Twitch during your dynasty week and Ball Boy announces it automatically in your game thread — no button, no command, within seconds of you going live.

**Setting it up:**
- **Link your Twitch** in the Activity (the stream step of the claim window).
- A commissioner turns it on: set a **streams channel** with **`/admin channels`**, and configure at least one **keyword** with **`/admin stream`** (e.g. your league name or the game you're playing).

Ball Boy announces only when it's really you playing your dynasty: your stream title matches a configured keyword, you own a team, and you have an unplayed game that week. Your matchup and stream link are posted right in the game thread for everyone to jump in.
