# v1.0.59 — Bowl Games Go In The Week You Put Them

_Released 2026-08-16_

## What's New

**🏈 Every bowl gets its own week**

Each bowl in the Schedule activity now has a **Bowl Week 1 / Bowl Week 2** picker. Set it per game.

Before this, every non-playoff bowl was filed into Bowl Week 1 with no way to move it.

## Fixes

**↩️ Your week picks survive reopening the builder**

The builder used to read every existing bowl back as Bowl Week 1, so the next Apply quietly dragged your Week 2 games back to Week 1.

## Notes

The playoff is untouched — quarterfinal hosts stay Bowl Week 2, and the semifinals and championship are still built for you as teams advance. Bowl Weeks 3 and 4 aren't offered because those weeks belong to those rounds.
