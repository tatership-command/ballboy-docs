# v1.0.59 — Bowl Games Go In The Week You Put Them

_Released 2026-08-16_

## Bowl Season

**🏈 You can now say which week a bowl game is played in**

When you build your bowl slate in the Schedule activity, every bowl now has its own week picker — Bowl Week 1 or Bowl Week 2. Set it per game, the way your slate actually falls.

Before this, you couldn't. Every bowl game outside the playoff was filed into Bowl Week 1 no matter what, and there was no control anywhere on the screen to move one. If half your slate belonged in the second week, there was nothing you could do about it.

**↩️ Reopening the builder no longer undoes your work**

If you set a bowl to Bowl Week 2, applied it, then came back to the screen later, the builder used to forget — it read every existing bowl back in as Bowl Week 1, and the next Apply quietly dragged them all back. Your week assignments now survive that round trip.

**🤖 The playoff is unchanged**

Quarterfinal host bowls are still Bowl Week 2 automatically, and still aren't yours to move — that's how the bracket works. The Semifinals and the National Championship are still generated for you as teams advance. Nothing about those changed.

Bowl Weeks 3 and 4 aren't offered in the picker on purpose: those weeks belong to the Semifinals and the Championship.

## What's Next

Bowl week is the last piece of the bowl slate that wasn't yours to set. The rest of the Schedule activity is unchanged in this release.
