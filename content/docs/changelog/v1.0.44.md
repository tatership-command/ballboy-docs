# v1.0.44 — Team Role Positioning Fixes

_Released 2026-07-25_

## Fixes

**🎨 Claimed team roles land in the right spot**
When someone claimed a team, their new team or conference role was dropped at the very top of the role list instead of grouped with the rest — so a freshly-claimed conference (like the Sun Belt) could jump above everything. New roles now sort into the correct order automatically on every claim.

**🛠️ `/admin roles_sync_all` no longer gets stuck**
Role sync could refuse to run — "Move Ball Boy's role above its team & conference roles" — even when Ball Boy was already above every team and conference role, because it was also counting your Admin/Commissioner roles. It now repositions every role it can and simply leaves in place any role it can't move (like an Admin role sitting above the bot), telling you exactly which ones it skipped instead of stopping.
