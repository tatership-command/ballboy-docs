# v1.0.61 — Game Availability & Timezones

_Released 2026-08-20_

## New

**🗓️ Availability — find a time that works for both of you**

Hit **Add Availability** in any game thread and enter when you're free. Ball Boy
works out where you and your opponent overlap, and either of you can propose a
kickoff from a shared window — the other confirms, and the game is scheduled.

**🕐 Timezones, handled**

Your timezone is detected automatically the first time you open the Activity, and
shows under your team in every new game thread. Every time Ball Boy displays now
renders in **your** local timezone, so nobody does the mental arithmetic.

Set or correct it any time with `/timezone` — all 597 zones, with type-ahead.

**🏫 Full school names**

New seasons use the full name — *Western Michigan*, not *W. Michigan*. Existing
seasons are unchanged.

## Fixes

`/game schedule_time` now offers every timezone instead of ten. A failed profile
read during a team claim no longer overwrites saved connections or stream links.
