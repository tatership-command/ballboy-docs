# v1.0.46 — Smarter Stream Auto-Detection

_Released 2026-07-29_

## What's New

**🎯 Smarter stream matching.** Ball Boy's Twitch auto-detection now confirms it's *really your dynasty game* before announcing — so streaming a different league (or a different week) never trips a false alert.

A commissioner controls what has to match with **`/admin stream`**:
- **League name** — your stream title mentions the league (on by default).
- **Team names** — your title names at least one of the two teams playing (on by default).
- **Week** — your title says the week (`week 5`, `wk 5`, `week5`, `wk5`).
- **Keyword** — a custom phrase you set.

Every signal you turn on must match, so a "going live" alert only fires when the stream is unmistakably for the game you're about to play in *this* league. Turn signals on or off anytime to make matching as strict or as loose as you want.
