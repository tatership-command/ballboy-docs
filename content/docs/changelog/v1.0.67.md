# v1.0.67 — Team Logos Render Full Size

_Released 2026-08-27_

## Fixes

**🖼️ Team logos were rendering at about 40% size in generated images**

Every logo Ball Boy draws into an image — the champion hero, the CFP and
conference-championship game-thread heroes, and the schedule matrix — was being
padded onto a wide banner canvas before it was drawn, which shrank the logo and
pushed it off-centre inside its slot.

They now render at full size. This affects newly generated images only; ones
already posted keep the old look.

**🏆 Champion hero polish**

The year now reads as white digits with a gold outline, and the FINAL label in
the score bar matches its intended spacing.
