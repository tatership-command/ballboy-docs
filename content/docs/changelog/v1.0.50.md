# v1.0.50 — Stream Link Fixes

_Released 2026-08-05_

## Fixes

**🔗 Stream links copied from Share buttons now work.** If your YouTube or Twitch link was pasted straight from the app's Share button or a phone browser, Ball Boy could not read your channel from it — so your streams were never announced, with nothing to indicate anything was wrong.

Ball Boy now understands the everyday forms those links come in:
- YouTube Share links with the `?si=…` tag on the end
- Mobile links like `m.twitch.tv/…`
- Links with `www.`, a trailing slash, or extra `?`/`#` bits

**Already affected? You don't need to do anything.** Any link that was stuck is picked up automatically within about 15 minutes of this update — no need to re-link your account.
