# v1.0.63 — Conference Dimming Fix

_Released 2026-08-21_

## Fixes

**🏟️ Move here buttons now follow what you've actually selected**

On the conference screen, once you'd staged one move, selecting a different set
of teams didn't update which **Move here** buttons were greyed out — they kept
reflecting the previous batch. They now always match your current selection.
