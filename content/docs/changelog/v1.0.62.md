# v1.0.62 — Conference Management

_Released 2026-08-21_

## New

**🏟️ Rebuild your conferences in one screen**

`/conference manage` now opens an Activity screen showing **every conference at
once**. Click schools wherever they sit — across as many conferences as you like
— then tap **Move here** on the destination. Nothing saves until you apply, so a
half-finished realignment can be thrown away.

**🏆 Championship games can pull a team in**

Any team can be picked for a title game, not just that conference's current
members. An out-of-conference pick is flagged next to the team, and applying
moves them into the conference before creating the game. Importing a
championship CSV works the same way — it previews the moves and waits for you to
confirm.

## Fixes

**🔒 Team-logo route hardened**

A crafted request to the logo proxy could reach paths outside the logo bucket.
Now rejected.

Availability windows you're currently inside no longer disappear from "Both
free", and the conference championship warning names the offending team instead
of pointing at the wrong slot.
