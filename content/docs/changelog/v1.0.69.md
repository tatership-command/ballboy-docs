# v1.0.69 — Home & Away Counts While You Build

_Released 2026-08-27_

## New

**🏠 Each team's home/away split, right on its card**

Open **Manage Schedule → Regular Season** in the Activity and every team's card
now shows its home and away totals beside the games count — 🏠 6 · ✈️ 6.

They update as you edit. Flip a home/away toggle and both teams recount
straight away, so a lopsided schedule is visible while you're still fixing it
rather than after you apply. Byes count toward neither side, matching the
weekly schedule image.

Thanks to **kawika_wika** for suggesting it.
