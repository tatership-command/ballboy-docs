# v1.0.66 — National Champion Hero

_Released 2026-08-26_

## New

**🏆 A champion image when the national title is decided**

When the CFP National Championship settles, Ball Boy now posts a celebration
image alongside the usual result: the CFP trophy, the champion's logo and
record, the final score against the runner-up, and the manager who owns them.

It lands in the game thread and your **game results** channel attached to the
result post, and again in **league updates** as its own announcement.

The background is tinted with the champion's own team colors — darkened so the
gold and white stay readable, whatever the school's palette is.

**🖼️ `/admin champion_hero_preview`**

Owners and admins can render the image for any team on demand, without waiting
for a title game.
