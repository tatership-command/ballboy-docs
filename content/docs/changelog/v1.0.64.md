# v1.0.64 — Preseason & Offseason Readiness

_Released 2026-08-26_

## What's New

**🏈 Preseason gets its own announcement**

Starting a new season — with `/season create`, or by advancing past the
offseason — now posts a **Preseason** announcement carrying the **Ready to
Advance** button. Set up your recruiting board, scout your prospects, then mark
yourself ready so the commissioner can see who's done before the season kicks
off.

**✅ Ready to Advance on two more weeks**

**End of Season Recap** and **Training Results** now carry the Ready to Advance
button too, because both weeks have real work in them. Their activity text says
so now: roster and NIL retention on the recap week, and skill-point allocation
before it auto-locks on training results.
